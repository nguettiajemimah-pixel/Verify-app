Help & Information

How Verification Works

VERIFY GH helps you compare product information against available registry records. The process is simple:

  1. Enter the registration number, product name, and manufacturer from the product packaging
  2. We compare your submitted information with available registry records
  3. You see exactly which fields match or differ
  4. If you find an inconsistency, you can submit a report to the relevant authority
Understanding Verification Statuses

MATCH

The information you entered matches the available registry record for the checked fields.

PARTIAL MATCH

Some fields match, but others require attention or were not provided for comparison.

DOES NOT MATCH

One or more important fields differ from the available registry record.

NOT FOUND

No corresponding record was found in the available registry dataset. This does not prove that the product is fake. Further verification through the relevant authority may be appropriate.

Data Source Information

This prototype uses controlled demonstration data and is not a live official registry.

Verification results depend on the registry data available to the platform at the time of checking. VERIFY GH does not independently certify product authenticity, safety, or compliance.

Privacy Information

VERIFY GH collects only the information necessary to process your verification and report requests.

We do not require unnecessary personal information. Location information is collected at an appropriate level (town/district/region) unless more precise location is genuinely required.

About VERIFY GH

VERIFY GH helps consumers compare regulated-product information with available registry records. It does not independently certify authenticity, safety, or compliance.

We do not ask consumers to trust us blindly. We show you the information we compared, the source we used, what matched, what differed, and what you can do next.
