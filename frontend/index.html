How It Works

1. Enter Product Information
Enter the registration number, product name, and manufacturer from the product packaging.
2. Compare with Registry
We compare your submitted information against available registry records.
3. See What Matches
View exactly which fields match or differ from the registry record.
4. Report if Needed
If you find an inconsistency, submit a report to the relevant authority.

Verification Statuses

MATCH
The information you entered matches the available registry record.
PARTIAL MATCH
Some fields match, but others require attention or were not provided.
DOES NOT MATCH
One or more important fields differ from the registry record.
NOT FOUND
No corresponding record was found in the available registry dataset.
Important Notice

VERIFY GH compares product information with available registry records. It does not independently certify product authenticity or safety.

Verification results depend on the registry data available to the platform at the time of checking.
